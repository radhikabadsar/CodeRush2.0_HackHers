Privacy Architecture & Safety Boundaries

1. Automatic PII Redaction Layer

Scans input strings for phone numbers, email addresses, exact house numbers, and voter IDs, replacing them with sanitized placeholders prior to storage.

2. Geo-Spatial Location Fuzzing

Resident home locations are snapped to public civic landmarks (e.g. "Near Ward 12 Main Square") on public maps to prevent doxxing.

3. Non-Blocking Clarification Prompts

Incomplete complaints prompt clarification while allowing urgent safety hazards to immediately trigger escalation pipelines.

4. Immutable Audit Trail

Every routing decision, SLA timer update, human override, or status shift creates a cryptographically timestamped log entry.
